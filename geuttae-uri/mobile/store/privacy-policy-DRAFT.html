What the app does

This private photo-quiz app lets people who know one another create invitation-only gatherings. It has no registered user account system. A host approves entry requests.

Information processed

We process a nickname, room name, resized photos chosen for sharing, manually written questions and stories, answers, scores, temporary player/session IDs, and safety-report records to operate and protect a gathering. We do not extract GPS metadata from uploaded photo copies. Original photos remain on the user's device. We do not infer personal memories from a photo.

Where data goes

The mobile app sends the selected information over HTTPS to the app's server, hosted by Railway. Authorized room participants receive the content needed for the current game. Saved memory books contain only entries whose author separately allowed saving. There is no public photo feed or public room directory. This is access-controlled hosting, not end-to-end encryption. Hosting providers may process technical network/access records for service delivery and security; their configured retention and backup practices must be checked before the final policy is issued.

Retention and deletion

Temporary server photo copies are deleted when a gathering ends, when their author is removed by the host, or when the owner removes/replaces their question. Abandoned rooms expire no later than 24 hours after creation. Expired access is blocked immediately; physical deletion may be delayed by outages or storage failures and is retried. Room state and report metadata remain within the room's lifetime. Selecting a replacement photo or removing your question deletes the no-longer-used server copy. Exported PDFs/HTML books stay on the recipient's device and cannot be recalled by the app. The language preference is kept in local app settings; room keys are kept in the current session, not a registered account.

Permissions and optional features

Photos are selected through the system file chooser. The app does not request broad photo-library, camera, microphone or location access. The first candidate has no advertising or analytics SDK, live payment processing, or active AI photo/question service. Future changes require updated notices and disclosures.

Safety and consent

Share only content you have permission to use, including consent from people shown. Sexual content, child exploitation, hate, violence, harassment, unlawful content and privacy violations are prohibited. Hiding a photo affects only that participant's view and the memory book delivered to them; it can be reversed while viewing that question. Reporting requires a reason and final confirmation, records a temporary room report and hides the photo for the reporter. It does not end the game or automatically remove anyone. Only the host can remove a participant, in the lobby or during play; their questions and temporary photos are deleted and that room session loses access. A removal can end play if fewer than two participants remain. These controls do not implement a global identity/device ban or personal user blocking. Reports do not automatically send email. For operator review, email the room code and approximate time to the contact below.

Contact

Customer support, safety reports and privacy/deletion requests: keetojt@gmail.com. Include the room code and approximate time when available; do not send sensitive photos by email.

원본은 기기에 남고 축소 사본은 HTTPS로 Railway의 앱 서버에 전송합니다. 저장을 따로 허용한 내용만 추억집에 포함합니다. 모임 종료나 방장의 강퇴 시 해당 임시 사진을 삭제하며 방은 생성 후 최대 24시간에 만료됩니다. 장애가 있으면 물리 삭제를 재시도하지만 만료된 정보 접근은 먼저 차단합니다. 직접 내려받은 추억집은 각 기기에 남고 원격으로 회수하지 못합니다.

언어 선택만 기기 설정에 저장하고 방 접근 키는 현재 세션에 보관합니다. 첫 후보 버전에는 광고·분석 SDK, 실제 결제 또는 활성 AI 생성 서비스가 없습니다. 사진 숨기기는 본인의 화면과 전달받는 추억집에만 적용되며 현재 문제에서 다시 표시할 수 있습니다. 신고는 사유와 최종 확인을 거쳐 방의 임시 기록에 남고 신고자에게만 사진을 숨깁니다. 신고만으로 게임 종료나 자동 강퇴를 하지 않습니다. 방장만 대기방과 게임 중 참가자를 강퇴할 수 있고, 해당 참가자의 문제·임시 사진을 삭제하고 방 세션 접근을 막습니다. 남은 참가자가 두 명 미만이면 게임이 종료됩니다. 계정 전체·기기 전체 차단이나 개인별 사용자 차단 기능은 없습니다. 신고 이메일은 자동 발송되지 않으며 운영자 검토가 필요하면 아래 문의처로 방 코드와 발생 시각을 보내 주세요.
