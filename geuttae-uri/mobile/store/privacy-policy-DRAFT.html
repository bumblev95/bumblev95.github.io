What the app does

This private photo-quiz app lets people who know one another create invitation-only gatherings. It has no registered user account system. A host approves entry requests.

Information processed

We process a nickname, room name, resized photos chosen for sharing, manually written questions and stories, answers, scores, temporary player/session IDs, and safety-report records to operate and protect a gathering. We do not extract GPS metadata from uploaded photo copies. Original photos remain on the user's device. We do not infer personal memories from a photo.

Where data goes

The mobile app sends the selected information over HTTPS to the app's server, hosted by Railway. Authorized room participants receive the content needed for the current game. Saved memory books contain only entries whose author separately allowed saving. There is no public photo feed or public room directory. This is access-controlled hosting, not end-to-end encryption. Hosting providers may process technical network/access records for service delivery and security; their configured retention and backup practices must be checked before the final policy is issued.

Retention and deletion

Temporary server photo copies are deleted when a gathering ends or is reported. Abandoned rooms expire no later than 24 hours after creation. Expired access is blocked immediately; physical deletion may be delayed by outages or storage failures and is retried. Room state and report metadata remain within the room's lifetime. Selecting a replacement photo or removing your question deletes the no-longer-used server copy. Exported PDFs/HTML books stay on the recipient's device and cannot be recalled by the app. The language preference is kept in local app settings; room keys are kept in the current session, not a registered account.

Permissions and optional features

Photos are selected through the system file chooser. The app does not request broad photo-library, camera, microphone or location access. The first candidate has no advertising or analytics SDK, live payment processing, or active AI photo/question service. Future changes require updated notices and disclosures.

Safety and consent

Share only content you have permission to use, including consent from people shown. Sexual content, child exploitation, hate, violence, harassment, unlawful content and privacy violations are prohibited. In-app reporting ends the gathering and hides/deletes its temporary photos. A reported author's session is blocked in that room. These are room controls, not a global identity or device ban.
