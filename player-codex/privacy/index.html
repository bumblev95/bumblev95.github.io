Privacy & advertising

PLAYER’S CODEX is an independent fan guide for AION 2 and ArcheAge Chronicles. You do not need an account to read the public guides and news listing.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process IP addresses and request logs. Links to Steam, YouTube, publishers and other websites are covered by their own privacy policies.

Google AdSense

Public hub, game introduction and news pages include the Google AdSense Auto ads code and publisher verification tag. Ad serving depends on Google approving the site and the account’s ad settings. Advertising partners may process request, cookie and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse.

Advertising consent defaults to denied. Where required, a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) separately manages advertising choices. Analytics permission does not constitute advertising consent. Consult the relevant game tool pages for their own storage disclosures.

Your choices and contact

Manage personalized ads in Google Ads Settings. Read Google’s partner-site data policy and the AION 2 privacy notice for its browser tools and analytics.

Corrections or privacy questions can go to public GitHub Issues. Do not include private credentials or sensitive information in public issues.
