LATEST PATCH · NOV 12, 2026

What to do differently after the patch

November 12 live Early Access maintenance, followed by previously applied November 7 and 4 fixes. Status means XLGAMES announced a deployed fix; PLAYER’S CODEX has not reproduced these issues in-game.

Official XLGAMES notice
Nov 12 · applied · P1

Meliot and Malchuk: progression recovery

XLGAMES reports that Collapsed Southern Ruins can be re-entered after defeating Meliot and leaving without collecting the reward. It also fixed quest progression blocked after defeating Malchuk and leaving his channel. These are separate from earlier Malchuk channel-exit fixes; in-game replays have not been verified.

Nov 12 · applied · P1

Party Kevin step and Ismelo dialogue

During Cliffs of Madness party play, the entrance Kevin interaction at the Talk to Edward step was fixed. Within the Hubbub progression after speaking with Ismelo was fixed; XLGAMES says previously blocked characters will have their progress restored. These are publisher-confirmed fixes, not independently reproduced quests.

Nov 12 · applied · P2

Tempering stats and unrefunded stat points

Incorrect Temper stats on certain weapons were corrected. Some stat points not returned after a reset were also addressed; XLGAMES said previously missing points would be restored during maintenance. Do not rank weapons by pre-patch stats or assume a measured damage change. The Nov 11 compensation plan is a separate announcement; actual delivery is unverified.

Nov 11 announcement · planned / delivery unverified · P2

Tempering compensation: check eligibility

XLGAMES announced Tempering Stones for characters holding affected equipment at +5 or higher at Nov 10, 2026, 11:30 PM PST. The amount depends on the equipment grade and Temper level; the gear will not be taken back. Distribution was planned in stages after maintenance, but individual receipt has not been verified. All players were also promised 3 Compensation Chest II (Gold, Vivid Weapon/Armor Tempering Stones and Hersander’s Dice Keys) in a future maintenance, plus a Unique cosmetic to be designed and distributed later. Do not claim these planned gifts were already received. Original developer letter ↗

Nov 12 · applied · P2

Raid resistance: Shock / Poison

Enduring Nightmare Celtak received substantially higher Shock resistance and Vanguard of Destruction substantially higher Poison resistance. Re-evaluate element-dependent rotations; the notice provides no exact resistance values, DPS impact or optimal replacement build.

Nov 12 · applied · P2

Boss loot and Marketplace restrictions

Unique boss-loot weapons are now obtainable only once per character. Tradable loot weapons gained equip-level requirements; minimum equipment listing prices changed, and 12 Rare Hereafter Accessories became non-tradable. Check item and listing screens before repeat farming or purchases.

Nov 12 · applied · P2

Item weights reduced

Crafting materials now weigh less. Costumes, tools, bags, Gliders, quest items, notes, keys, recipes, blueprints and Nui’s Tears were set to 0 weight; selected consumable weights also changed. The earlier Storage 200 → 1,000 increase remains a separate Nov 7 change. Exact item-by-item weights require a live UI check.

Nov 12 · applied · P2

Swift Slash and Celtak terrain route

Wavesword’s Swift Slash sometimes missing enemies was fixed. An environmental-object method allowing damage-free attacks on Enduring Nightmare Celtak was also fixed. Do not use footage of the old shortcut as a current-build boss strategy.

Nov 12 · applied · P3

Memory, housing and storage stability

XLGAMES reports reduced runtime memory usage and effect/sound stutter, optimized housing-placement memory usage, fixed occasional storage deposit/withdrawal failures and a character login blocker. These are published fixes, not proof that crashes or freezes no longer occur on every device.

Nov 12 · applied · P3

Merchant access, quest record and crafting

Great Raven shop access after A Cry in the Ruins was fixed. Defeating Monstrous Tristan now updates the Adventure Log, including previous eligible clears. Craftworkers now sell Golog’s Crystal Orb; mistakenly granted development items became Broken Iron Fragments (shop sale: 10,000 Gold). NPC locations remain unverified.

The Diamond Shores Exploration quest, Weeping Dead’s Doll, Gnoll’s Doll, Sunken Glory rubbing-item placement, Phantoms of the Recalling Grounds and Bloodstained Note interaction received fixes. Update the game in your Steam Library before retrying.

03 / POWER SYSTEMS

Progression loop: leveling alone misses these

1

Quests & exploration → EXP

The bulk of your XP is from quests and points of interest, not just mob kills.

2

Level-ups → stat points

One point adds one stat. Preview combat-stat changes before confirming an allocation.

3

Coilstones & bosses → trait points

Weapon-specific Traits can add skills or change existing skills. They do not come from leveling alone.

4

Nui Tears → maximum HP

Collect tears in the world and hand them in at Nui Statues. Later HP increases cost more tears.

5

Field bosses & chests → loot

Field bosses can reward gear and materials; chests may hold gear and crafting recipes.

6

Hersander's Tower → challenge

Solve each tower's offering riddle to earn rewards and unlock the Hereafter Challenge.

Early Access player report · Per-item yields unverified

04 / BOSS & DUNGEON

Five adjustments before retrying a boss

  1. Spend an attempt observing tells, attack ranges and follow-up hits.
  2. Practice one dependable defense—block, parry or dodge—before adding more actions.
  3. Recover and reposition after taking a hit; use committed attacks only during openings.
  4. If damage is low, check your weapon Traits, unspent stat points and gear first.
  5. Dungeons can be tackled solo or in a party of up to four. Try grouping for tough encounters.

Nov 12: Shock resistance increased for Enduring Nightmare Celtak and Poison resistance for Vanguard of Destruction. Do not assume pre-patch elemental DPS or Celtak terrain shortcuts still work. After a death, check F / G respawn controls; for Malchuk and party issues, see patches and workarounds. Gameplay outcomes remain unverified.

Early Access trading: your first wagon run

Player-recorded example from the November 3 launch build. Trading opened in Early Access.

Nov 12 official patch reduced crafting-material weights; earlier trade-video costs and payouts are historical examples, not fixed profits. Verify cargo weight, permits, destination reward and market rate in the live UI.

From quest to delivery

  1. After Home Sweet Home in Diamond Shores, pick up Foundations of Trade from Vida in Seaglass Harbor.
  2. At Seaglass Harbor Trading Post, choose your goods and destination. Check your Peace Trade Permits, wagon rental fee and free cargo slots before loading.
  3. Drive to Watcher’s Trading Post, check the reward type and market rate on the sale screen, then deliver the goods. Follow the quest prompt to turn it in to Vida.

Gold and material payouts

On this route, purchased Bluesalt Brotherhood Trade Goods paid gold, while crafted Standard Lumber Trade Goods paid Standard Additive, a crafting material.

The reward depends on the cargo and destination. If you are after gold, check the destination’s displayed reward before loading the wagon.

One recorded Nov 3 sale: Seven purchased goods cost 12,600 gold, wagon rental cost 100 gold, and the cargo sold for 17,804 gold at Watcher’s at a displayed rate of 105%. Check the current payout, permit limit and wagon capacity in your game.

06 / PARTY & PVP

Co-op, server choice & PvP channels

Agree on a server before making characters. Cross-server play and character transfers are unavailable during Early Access.

  1. Start in a peaceful/standard channel while learning the core combat loop.
  2. PvP is an opt-in Conflict/PvP channel. Check Nui Statue access and current switch restrictions in-game.
  3. The party leader's channel transfer button can appear only under certain conditions.
  4. Before moving to the next area, check the party and individual-key workaround.
