2. Install through Steam

  1. Sign in to Steam on your PC and open the official ArcheAge Chronicles store page.
  2. Once available, select Download Demo and install it. No separate application or selection process is required.
  3. Launch the installed demo from your Steam library.

If the button is missing, check the opening time above and refresh the store. Look for the demo download area separately from the main game’s release-date or wishlist notice. Check PC requirements on the store page.

Installation instructions: official demo announcement. Button availability and a completed installation still need hands-on confirmation.

4. What can you play?

The developers estimate roughly 2–3 hours of quests. This estimates quest length; a separate total playtime cap is not stated in the announcement.

  • Early-game content centered on Diamond Shores.
  • Four announced classes, with two demo weapons each.
  • A separate high-level dungeon and optional PvP channels.

Some life content, including housing and trade, is excluded. Demo progress and saves will not carry over to Early Access. The full game’s store description is not the demo’s content list.

Sources: latest demo details · initial announcement · classes, weapons and PvP.

5. Throne of Hinnon: find the hidden key

Official announcement

A high-level dungeon outside normal quest progression. Entry needs a hidden key; demo-level characters may find it extremely difficult.

Needs gameplay evidence

Key acquisition, the entrance route and boss responses still need PLAYER’S CODEX’s independent review. See the Korean community video for the creator’s guidance.

Source: Producer Ham’s letter. Capture boss footage under the existing dungeon verification target.

6. When can you enter PvP?

Official entry condition: leave the first town, then reach the Nui Statue at your next destination to access a PvP channel.

Regular channels do not allow players to attack each other; PvP channels offer player combat. Use this newer entry guidance instead of reading earlier “from the start” wording as immediate access at character creation.

Needs gameplay evidence: the actual statue location, switch menu, cooldown and effects. Record these under the existing regular/PvP channel target before publishing findings.

Sources: latest demo entry conditions · regular/PvP channel rules.

7. Changes since the network test

Developer-announced improvements; PLAYER’S CODEX has not independently tested their effectiveness.

  • Shared party quests and improved party channel matching.
  • Clearer dungeon/exploration clues and hint guidance.
  • Voice-over, facial animation and audio improvements.
  • Ladder collision, hit detection, translation and visual fixes.

Additional class gender options are in development, not in this demo.

Source: Producer Ham’s change summary.

Progression in brief · October 15 announcement

A short developer-described overview. Numbers, coordinates and build recommendations need gameplay evidence; not every progression system is confirmed for the demo.

  • Quests/discoveries → EXP and levels → attribute points.
  • Raeven’s Coilstones near bosses → separate trait points → weapon skills/variations.
  • Nui Tears → maximum HP at statues; exploration also rewards gear/materials.
  • Complete a zone’s tower riddles → unlock Hereafter Challenge.
